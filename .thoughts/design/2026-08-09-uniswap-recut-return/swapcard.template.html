What the re-cut changes

Same anatomy you accepted — four deliberate additions, each mapped to a real Uniswap/Jupiter pattern.

  • 1Real asset marks, not monograms. FXRP, FLR, XRP and FAssets render the official Flare SVG marks (MIT, already vendored in your repo). Assets without a bundled mark — USD₮0 here — fall back to a seeded monogram. Bundled → monogram, no network fetch, so it holds under a widget's strict CSP.
  • 2Swap / Limit tabs. One card, a segmented control, one shared currency context — Uniswap's structure. Limit adds a price row and expiry; the rest of the anatomy is untouched.
  • 3A connect flow that names both chains. EVM wallets (detected via EIP-6963, "Detected"/"Recent" badges) for the swap — and it says plainly that FAssets mint/redeem also links an XRPL wallet. Uniswap's modal never has to admit two chains; ours does.
  • 4Palette untouched. Every colour is still your accepted paper + cobalt. The logos are the only new colour, framed in your circle — real marks without becoming a full-colour crypto-dashboard wall.
