BridgeCard

The same spine as the swap, with three differences that matter: a chain on each leg, an itemised fee breakdown instead of slippage, and a two-legged outcome. Source final with destination pending is a real, stable state, not an error.

import { BridgeCard } from "@flare-kit/react-ui"

Mock by default. No wallet, no chain, no testnet funds. Walk the states to see the in-flight and partial outcomes, which is where most bridge UIs give up and show a spinner.

What differs from a swap

A chain sits on each leg, and the flip control swaps both the chains and the assets. Slippage is replaced by an arrival estimate, because a bridge does not slip, it waits. Fees are itemised rather than rolled into one number: the messaging fee, the destination gas someone must pay on the far side, and the executor.

The recipient is editable. Bridging to an address you do not control on the destination chain is the single most expensive mistake in this category, so the address is shown on the leg rather than buried in settings.

The partial outcome

This is the state the whole kit exists for. Source is final, destination has not completed, and neither success nor failure is true. We name it, say exactly what moved, and offer only actions that cannot double-spend.

  • submitted source transaction broadcast
  • awaiting_external message in flight, provider named
  • partially_succeeded delivered, compose outstanding
  • action_required retry or refund, provider-valid only
  • succeeded destination credited
  • unknown provider unreachable, never shown as failed
