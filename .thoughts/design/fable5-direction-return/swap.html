SwapCard

A swap you already know how to use. The layout follows the convention every trader has muscle memory for; what differs is that the venue is named, the allowance is exact, and the swap continues into a durable operation you can recover.

import { SwapCard } from "@flare-kit/react-ui"

Anatomy

Top to bottom, in the order people expect: header with settings, sell leg, flip control, buy leg, detail block, one primary button. Each leg carries its own label, amount, fiat sub-value, balance and MAX. Insufficient balance is shown on the leg itself, not only on the button.

MAX reserves gas. On the sell leg, MAX leaves enough native FLR to pay for the transaction. Selling the native token and being unable to submit it is a bug, not an edge case.

Button states

One button, evaluated in this order. The label is the state machine, so a user never has to guess why they cannot continue.

  • Connect wallet no signer
  • Select a token leg incomplete
  • Enter an amount no input
  • Finding a route quoting
  • Insufficient FXRP amount exceeds balance
  • Not enough FLR for gas native balance too low
  • Approve exactly 100 FXRP allowance required
  • Quote changed, review again terms moved
  • Review swap ready

What we do differently

The venue is named. We say SparkDEX and show its health, and we say only qualified route found rather than best route when comparison coverage is incomplete. Claiming best requires having checked everything.

The allowance is exact. The button reads Approve exactly 100 FXRP, never an unlimited approval by default.

The swap does not end at submission. onOperation hands you a durable operation, so a swap that reverts, partially fills or stalls lands on the same timeline and recovery surface as every other operation in the kit.
