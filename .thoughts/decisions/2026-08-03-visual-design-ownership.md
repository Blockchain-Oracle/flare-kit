# Decision: External Visual-Design Ownership

Date: 2026-08-03  
Status: canonical current design-ownership decision  
Mode: `EXTERNAL_COMMISSION`  
Named owner: Claude Fable 5

## Decision

The participant assigned visual and interaction design for the Flare Application Layer to Claude
Fable 5. The context-holding Codex agent owns the product-context packet, traceability and return
audit; it does not choose the visual language.

The accepted [product-surface map](../design/2026-08-03-product-surface-map.md) remains the floor for
navigation, screens, states, data, generated artifacts and copy laws. Fable may propose useful
connective additions marked `DISCOVERED`, but may not remove mapped coverage, redefine product
scope, change authority or claim that a design prototype proves a production integration.

## Authority And Approval

- Abu retains final taste authority and accepts, rejects or requests a re-cut of the visual
  direction.
- Claude Fable 5 owns visual language, interaction treatment and design expression within the
  accepted product contract.
- Codex packages context and audits the return against the commission and surface map.
- No visual direction is accepted merely because it was delivered.
- No design artifact authorizes production architecture, framework selection, implementation,
  deployment, credentials or value-moving actions.

## Commission And Transfer

- Durable commission: [Claude Fable 5 designer commission](../design/2026-08-03-designer-commission.md).
- Paste-ready prompt: [Claude Fable 5 operating prompt](../design/2026-08-03-designer-agent-prompt.md).
- Expected first-return directory: `.thoughts/design/fable5-direction-return/`.
- First return: representative visual-language direction only, not all 112 surfaces.
- Later delivery: coherent batches of three to four screens or state groups after Abu accepts the
  direction.

## Next Gate

Deliver the prompt and commission to Claude Fable 5, receive the first direction return, then audit
it with the Direction Return Gate before Abu's taste decision. Architecture and implementation
remain blocked until accepted design evidence is converted into a design document.
