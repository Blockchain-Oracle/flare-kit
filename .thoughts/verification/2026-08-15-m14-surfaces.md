## One finding, pre-existing and kit-wide — NOT M14's

The gallery's own auditor reports the primary `Button` as `not-measurable` because it paints
over a gradient, and asks for it to be checked by hand. Measured:

| Theme | top stop | 60% stop | bottom stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 4.61:1 | 5.66:1 | 7.42:1 |
| **Dark** | 4.61:1 | **2.70:1** | **2.05:1** |

The label is 16px/600, which is **not** WCAG "large text" (that needs ≥18.66px bold), so the
4.5:1 threshold applies. **In dark theme the lower two-thirds of every primary button in the kit
fails AA.**

It is not an M14 regression: measured identically on M13's and M5's primary buttons, because it
is the shared `Button` primitive. AC6 asks for zero *new* findings and M14 adds none — but this
was previously unmeasured rather than passing, and it is now measured and failing.

**Left unfixed deliberately.** The fix is a `DESIGN.md` decision (change the gradient stops, the
label colour, or add a text shadow) and it would alter the primary control on every screen in
every milestone, including their already-verified screenshots. That is Abu's call, not a change
to make inside M14's scope.
