## Six defects the screenshots found that the tests did not

Every one of these passed 113 green tests before being seen.

1. **Stale `ConnectButton` CSS survived the migration.** `.fk-accounts`,
   `.fk-account`, `.fk-account-chain` and `.fk-account-id` were still declared
   in `compose.css` after the component was deleted, and silently fought the
   new rules in `accounts.css` — `display: inline-flex` won, centring every
   card's contents. Removed. This is exactly what CLAUDE.md's "delete dead code
   as you migrate" exists to prevent, and deleting the `.tsx` was not enough.

2. **Two icons had no CSS class.** `icon="eye"` and `icon="globe"` were used by
   `AccountSheet` and `NetworkResolutionSheet`; `primitives.css` defined neither.
   `.fk-i` sets `background: currentColor` and masks it with `--fk-icon`, so an
   undefined icon renders as a **solid black square**, not as nothing. Added
   `.fk-i-eye` and `.fk-i-globe` against the already-vendored `eye.svg` and
   `globe-simple.svg`.

3. **A wrong-network card offered "Connect Flare".** The account was already
   connected — just on the wrong chain — so the one control on screen told a
   person to do the thing that would not help. `AccountSheet` now offers
   connecting only where connecting is the remedy, and `restored` says
   "Re-authorize" rather than "Connect". Wrong-network and account-changed are
   repaired in SH-03, which is the surface that knows what the drift
   invalidated.

4. **Dark theme looked half-applied — but the kit was right.** The light patches
   were the gallery's own chrome: `--fk-*` tokens are declared on `.fk`, and
   `.g-mount` sat outside that scope, so it silently used its light fallbacks.
   The kit surfaces were correct throughout. Fixed in the gallery by scoping its
   root to `.fk`. Worth recording because the first reading of that screenshot
   was wrong: the harness was misreporting the thing it was built to check.

5. **`PendingTray` rendered "1 need you".** A count templated straight into a
   sentence with no singular form. Now "One operation needs you" / "N operations
   need you", and the rule is recorded in `SPEC.md` because it applies to every
   future surface rather than only this one.

6. **The gallery could not Fast Refresh.** `main.tsx` both defined a component
   and called `createRoot`, so every edit full-reloaded and re-rooted the same
   container, warning each time. Split into `Gallery.tsx` (components) and
   `main.tsx` (mount only). A harness nobody can iterate in stops being used.

## Confirmed by eye, not only by assertion

- **R-DATA-003 holds visually.** A mint renders as three labelled groups —
  `XRP LEDGER`, `DATA CONNECTOR`, `FLARE` — each with its own copyable evidence
  chip. The ledger payment and the Flare execution are links to two different
  explorers; the attestation round is deliberately not a link, because no
  explorer indexes it.
- **M2-AC6 is legible.** SH-03 shows `Required` and `Current` as a pair, with
  the account named alongside, so a person can tell what to switch *back to*.
- **Exactness holds.** `120.500000000000000000 MFLR` renders at full stored
  precision in the mono face with tabular numerals, carrying its asset.
- **Unbuilt is declared, not empty.** "Not covered by this build" lists vault,
  bridge, delegation and stake with a reason each and no value column at all.
- Every mock value is visibly fake — `MFLR`, `MXRP`, `FMockXRP`, `0xM0CK…`,
  `rMOCKA…` — so no screenshot here can be mistaken for a live one.

## M1's four composed surfaces, examined

Added to the gallery and driven with real input, not just mounted.

- **`MintFXRP` (FX-02) — correct.** With `25.000000 XRP` typed: receive
  `23.500000 FMockXRP`, protocol fee `1.000000`, executor fee `0.500000`. Sums
  to the input exactly. AC7's refusal is the strongest copy in the product —
  below minimum it disables the button, relabels it **"Amount too small"**
  rather than leaving a generic verb, marks the field invalid, and says *"the
  protocol would take the entire amount as its fee and you would receive
  nothing back, with no way to recover it."* Zeroes in that state are true, not
  placeholders: you would receive nothing. "No executor named" is disclosed.
- **`RedeemFXRP` (FX-07) — correct.** Two lots: burn `20.000000 FMockXRP`, fee
  `0.100000`, receive `19.900000 XRP`. States **"Paid by: an agent, not the
  protocol"** and the collateral fallback in full — *"you can claim 105% of the
  value in collateral on Flare instead. You would receive collateral, not
  XRP."*
- **`RecoveryPanel` (SH-06) — correct.** Offers "Complete the mint" with the
  reuse guarantee stated, and renders "Nothing for you to do yet" rather than
  an empty panel when no safe action exists.

**One judgement I reversed.** `RecoveryPanel` states the reuse guarantee twice
— the action's own description ends "It sends no further XRP", then the panel
adds "It sends no further funds." I rewrote it as a short classification, and
two M1 tests failed (`timeline-states.test.tsx:89`, plus an accessibility one).
They pin both halves of that sentence deliberately. I reverted: this is the most
dangerous claim in the product, the redundancy is belt-and-braces on it, and
rewriting safety copy against its own tests at the end of a long session is not
a change to make quickly. The comment now records why it looks redundant, so the
next reader does not "fix" it either.
